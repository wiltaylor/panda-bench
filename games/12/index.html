How to play

Defeat all three pandas before their pineapples cost you all five hearts.

  • Sword — strike pandas at close range.
  • Pickaxe — break blocks and collect building materials.
  • Shield — select it to block incoming pineapples from the front.
  • Planks and stone — select a block and place it to build cover.

Desktop Use WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, left click to use a tool, and right click to place. Press 1–5 to select and Esc to pause.

Touch Move with the stick, drag the scene to look, tap the hotbar to equip an item, and use the on-screen action buttons.
