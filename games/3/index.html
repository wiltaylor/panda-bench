Pineapple Panda Siege

Goal: the pandas throw pineapples at you. Break blocks, build walls, and fight back.

Desktop: WASD move, Space jump, mouse look (click the game to lock the mouse), left click break / hit / fire, right click place a block or hold the shield, F holds the shield, keys 1-9 or the mouse wheel pick an item, Esc pauses.

Phone or tablet: left stick moves, drag the right side of the screen to look, Hit/Break and Place act on the block in the crosshair, hold Shield to block, Jump jumps. Tap a slot in the bar to pick an item.

Items: 1-6 blocks, 7 sword (close range), 8 slingshot (ranged), 9 shield (blocks pineapples in front of you).
