Waking the pandas…

A blocky survival sketch

PandaCraft

Pandas throw pineapples. Break and place blocks. Guard with the stone sword and send the fruit back.

Desktop

  • WASD move, mouse look
  • Space jump, Shift sneak on edges
  • Left click breaks or attacks
  • Right click places
  • Hold right click with the sword to guard
  • Keys 1–9 or the wheel pick the hotbar
  • Esc pauses, F3 shows colliders

Touch

  • Left stick moves
  • Drag the view to look
  • Jump, Break, and Place sit under your thumb
  • Place becomes Guard when the sword is selected
  • Tap the hotbar to switch tools

Paused

Walls stop pineapples. Leaves do not. A sword swing or a guard sends a pineapple back at the panda who threw it.
